# PESE Alpha 7 — Aggressive Learning BETA training update

This is an experimental update to the existing PESE Windows application. It keeps
the normal package identity, update channel, Brain pointer and canonical data.

**On first startup for each Brain and Windows account, you must accept the
training/privacy agreement to continue. Declining closes updated PESE without
enrolling you or collecting new research data.**

Do not include highly personal journals, passwords, financial records, private
correspondence, confidential documents, medical records or other sensitive
material. Use non-sensitive educational notes, study materials, public research
and technical documentation. Local processing does not make content risk-free.

Acceptance authorizes a snapshot of the current approved document collection,
whether it contains two, 200, 400 or more documents. Exclusions remain in effect.
Future document IDs require a separate explicit scope approval. Each Brain and
Windows account has separate consent and local experimental storage. Journals
remain off by default. Ordinary document indexing is independent of training
permissions. A verified local backup and inactive restore precede activation.

Bounded neural ranking processes at most six admitted search candidates while
MAAE evidence gates and deterministic answer grounding remain authoritative.
An active neural model is not an actively running training job. Search feedback
needs appropriate owner review before becoming trusted training labels. Candidate
fitting requires sufficient valid/diverse evidence and disjoint evaluation;
promotion requires the existing quality/resource gates. Rollback and MAAE fallback
remain available. Missing or incompatible models fall back to MAAE.

Raw events, queued examples and reviewed labels use seven-day retention by default.
Derived job datasets and private checkpoints use the configured file-age window.
Cleanup occurs during local app maintenance; it is postponed while PESE is closed.
Newer models can retain older patterns. Expiry and document/example deletion do
not guarantee selective removal of learned influence. Consent, scope and operational
receipts remain until Clear. Documents, ordinary Search Sync feedback, saved
history, the packaged base, existing backups and copies elsewhere are outside this
policy. Exiting stops new collection/training; Clear removes this account's BETA
queue, reviewed labels, job datasets and private checkpoints, preserving documents.
Privacy settings and exclusions stay on device with BETA disabled. In this training
update, decline or exit closes PESE.

Data is not automatically transmitted to Maniac Softwares. Private content,
queries, embeddings and private model updates are not automatically uploaded.
External sharing requires separate explicit authorization. Google Drive remains
optional explicit canonical backup; experimental sidecars do not roam in snapshots.

The packaged synthetic base remains unchanged. Independent improvement on real
owner searches has not been established. Experimental quality and cold-start
behavior can vary; this release does not claim general English understanding.
